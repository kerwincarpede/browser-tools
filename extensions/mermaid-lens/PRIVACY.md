# Mermaid Lens privacy policy

_Last updated: 14 November 2026_

Mermaid Lens does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

- **What it reads:** On sites where it is enabled (github.com and gist.github.com
  by default, plus any site you turn it on for), it reads the Mermaid diagram
  source on the page so it can redraw the diagram with extra controls. This happens
  entirely in your browser.
- **What it stores:** Your preferences (highlight colour and mode, animation,
  theme, and the sites you've switched it off for), stored with
  `chrome.storage.sync` so they follow your browser profile. Nothing else is stored.
- **Network:** The extension makes no network requests of its own. It sends
  diagram code to a third party only when you choose **Open in Mermaid Live
  Editor**, which opens mermaid.live with that diagram in a new tab.
- **Clipboard:** It writes to the clipboard only when you click a copy action. It
  never reads your clipboard.

Questions: open an issue on this extension's GitHub repository.
